nonstandardIngredients:
  - ingredient: Semolina flour
    note: 'is coarser and higher-protein than all-purpose or even bread flour, milled from durum wheat rather than common wheat. It adds a faint golden color and a pleasant chew, but at only 20% of the blend here it stays a supporting player rather than taking over the crumb''s structure.'
  - ingredient: Gochujang
    note: 'is a sticky, fermented Korean chili paste -- closer in texture to a thick jam than a spice. Folding it evenly through the whole dough would over-hydrate and over-color the crumb, so this recipe treats it the way you''d treat cinnamon filling in a swirl loaf: spread and rolled in at shaping, not mixed in with everything else.'
  - ingredient: Gruyère
    note: 'is a semi-hard cheese that holds its shape reasonably well under oven heat compared to a soft, young cheese -- but it still needs to go in cold, to avoid smearing into the dough instead of staying in distinct melty pockets.'
  - ingredient: Scallions
    note: 'are mostly water. Added too early, they can macerate and turn a little bitter over a long bulk fermentation -- this recipe folds them in partway through bulk instead of at the very start, and keeps the quantity modest so they read as bright flecks rather than a soggy vegetable.'
